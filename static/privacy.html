Privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Mteja Insights, Nairobi, Kenya · mtejainsights@gmail.com

This policy explains how Mteja Insights handles personal data under the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya). We act in two roles:

  • Controller of Merchant data: the businesses and people who sign up.
  • Processor of Customer data: messages and payment messages of a Merchant’s own customers. The Merchant decides why that data is collected and is your first contact if you are their customer.

1. Data we handle

GroupWhat
MerchantName, business name, email, phone, role, login details, billing records, usage and device logs, support messages.
GhostDemand customer dataMessages and enquiries from connected channels, sender phone or email, name if given, time, products asked about, and the extracted category and reason not fulfilled.
PesaShield customer dataM-Pesa confirmation messages: transaction code, amount, date and time, sender name (often masked) and number, receiving till, paybill or number.

We never ask for M-Pesa PINs, passwords or card numbers. We do not intentionally process children’s data or sensitive personal data.

3. Automated tools

GhostDemand and PesaShield use automated analysis, including machine learning. They support a Merchant’s decision. Under section 35 of the Act, you have the right not to be subject to a significant decision based solely on automated processing, so Merchants must have a person review doubtful results.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it with the Merchant concerned, service providers under contract (cloud hosting, messaging and email delivery, analytics and AI, billing, support), channel providers such as WhatsApp when you connect them, regulators and courts where the law requires, and a successor if the business is sold.

5. Transfers outside Kenya

Some providers may store data abroad. We transfer data only as sections 48 to 50 of the Act and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021 allow, using adequacy, safeguards or consent.

6. How long we keep it

  • Merchant account data: while active, then up to 24 months.
  • Billing and tax records: at least 7 years.
  • Customer messages and PesaShield results: the period the Merchant sets (default 12 and 24 months), then deleted or anonymised.
  • Security logs: 12 months.

7. Security and breaches

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, multi-factor authentication, logging, backups and vendor checks. If a breach puts people at risk, we tell the Merchant within 48 hours, and where we are controller we notify the Data Commissioner within 72 hours and affected people without unreasonable delay.

8. Your rights

You may be informed, access your data, object, correct it, erase it, move it, and withdraw consent at any time. For Merchant data, email mtejainsights@gmail.com. For customer data, contact the business you dealt with; if you write to us we will pass it on and help, usually within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

9. Cookies, children and changes

We use essential cookies for sign-in and security, and others only with consent. The Services are for adults and businesses. We will give 30 days’ notice of material changes to this policy.
