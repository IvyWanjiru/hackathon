Notice for your customers

Show this on your website, checkout or WhatsApp welcome message, and adapt the details.

How [Your business] uses your information

When you message us or pay us, we collect your name, phone number or email, your messages, and M-Pesa payment details (transaction code, amount, time, sender name and number).

  • To serve you: reply, confirm stock and prices, take orders and deliver.
  • To confirm your payment quickly: our service provider Mteja Insights runs a tool called PesaShield that checks your M-Pesa message against our records. This is necessary to complete your purchase.
  • To learn what customers want: a tool called GhostDemand analyses messages to see which products people ask for and why a request could not be met. This is optional and you can still buy if you decline.

Only we and Mteja Insights see your data, which is processed on our instructions. We do not sell it. A person on our team decides when there is a problem with a payment or order. You can see, correct or delete your data, object, or withdraw consent by contacting us, and you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Never send your M-Pesa PIN or password. We will never ask for it.
